# BM25 keyword search

BM25 is a ranking function that scores chunks by the query words they contain. Rare words count for more than common words, and repeated words give diminishing returns. The usual parameters are k1 of 1.5, which limits the gain from repeated terms, and b of 0.75, which normalizes for chunk length.

BM25 is strong when the query contains exact identifiers such as error codes, product names, or function names, because dense embeddings often blur those tokens. It is weak when the question uses different words than the document, such as asking about automobiles when the text says cars.

That opposite weakness is why dense and keyword search are combined in hybrid retrieval.
